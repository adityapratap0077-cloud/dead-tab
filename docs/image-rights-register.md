## Rules

- Old, famous, or widely copied does not mean free to use.
- Screenshots of historical websites are reproduced only with verified permission or a clearly applicable licence; otherwise we describe and link.
- A takedown or correction request from a rights holder is actioned promptly and noted in `docs/correction-log.md`.
