The archive constellation

The museum, mapped as a sky

Every exhibit is a star. The threads between them are the museum's real cross-links, the same related exhibits you will find at the bottom of each case file. Drag to turn the sky. Click a star to go there.

Star colours follow the evidence scale: green verified, blue documented, amber partially documented. If the sky does not render on your device, the same map in words: DT-001 Before the Feed · DT-002 Orkut · DT-003 GeoCities · DT-004 Cicada 3301 (open case).

One exhibit is never just one exhibit

Every case in this museum ends with threads to pull: a related site, a neighbouring era, a question nobody has answered. That is deliberate. The early internet was not a feed you scrolled; it was a maze you wandered. We have kept the maze.

The museum is built from memories like yours

We do not run a paid newsletter, and we will not ask for your email to fake one. New exhibits are announced on the founders' LinkedIn pages, and this site carries a simple RSS feed you can add to any reader. Bookmark the archive; the archive will be here.
