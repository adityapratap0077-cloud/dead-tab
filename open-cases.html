Open cases

Investigations, not accusations

An open case is a question the internet never finished answering. Each case file separates what is established, what others claim, what evidence exists, what is disputed, what else could explain it, and what remains unknown. We publish conclusions only as strongly as the evidence allows.

How to read a case file

Established facts are sourced like any other exhibit. Claims are attributed to whoever made them and are never upgraded to facts by repetition. Unknowns stay unknown until evidence arrives. And a standing rule: this museum never tries to identify, contact, or expose private individuals. Curiosity ends where a person's safety begins.
