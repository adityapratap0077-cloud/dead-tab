Privacy

Privacy, exactly as it works here

DEAD TAB is a static website. That simple fact decides most of this page.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. There are no accounts, no cookies set by us, no analytics, no comment system, and no form on this site that stores your input. Search and filtering happen entirely inside your browser, on your device; what you type there is never sent anywhere.

What the host may collect

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub's servers necessarily process connection data (such as IP addresses) to serve pages and to keep their service secure, under GitHub's own privacy statement. We do not receive visitor analytics from GitHub Pages, and we will not claim audience numbers we cannot see.

Contributions

Submissions reach us through LinkedIn messages, which are governed by LinkedIn's privacy terms. What you send for the museum is used only to research and, with your permission, publish your contribution, credited as you choose. We do not sell or share submission details, and you can ask us to withdraw a contribution at any time.

External links

Exhibits link to sources and archives on other sites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

Changes

If this site ever adds analytics, a form service, or any feature that changes what is collected, this page will be updated before or at the same time as the change, and the change will say what data is involved and why.
