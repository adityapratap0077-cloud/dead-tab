Contribute

Submit a memory

The early internet lives in people's heads as much as in any archive. If you remember a place, a ritual, or a moment that this museum should preserve, we want to hear it.

What we accept

Forgotten websites

A site that mattered and is gone, dying, or transformed beyond recognition. A name and roughly when it lived are enough to start.

Internet memories

Your own recollection: a cybercafé ritual, a first username, a community you belonged to. Memories are published as recollections, in your voice, with your permission.

Screenshots and artifacts

Historical screenshots or files, only where you have the right to share them. Tell us where they came from; provenance is part of the exhibit.

Corrections

Found an error in an exhibit? Corrections with a source are the fastest way to make this museum better, and they are credited in the correction log.

Digital folklore

Forwards, chain messages, urban legends, and formats that travelled. If everyone you knew received it, it is folklore, and it belongs here.

Interview suggestions

Someone who ran, built, or lived inside a piece of internet history and might be willing to talk about it.

How submission works right now

This museum runs on free, static hosting with no database and no paid form service, so this page does not collect or store anything itself, and we will not pretend otherwise. Instead:

  1. Download the submission template and fill it in: submission-template.txt.
  2. Send it to either founder on LinkedIn: Aditya Pratap or Nandini Raj.
  3. Every submission is read and reviewed by a founder before anything is published. We may come back with questions, especially about dates and sources.
  4. If your memory is published, it appears as a labelled recollection with the credit name you choose in the template.

Please do not send

Private messages or personal data belonging to other people, material you do not have the right to share, or anything that identifies a private individual in a way that could harm them. The museum preserves cultures, not targets.
